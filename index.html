Last Updated: November 2025

MART Mail-Generator is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our temporary email service.

securityInformation We Collect

We are proud to operate a zero-data collection service:

  • No Personal Information: We don't collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • No Usage Analytics: We don't track your browsing behavior or service usage patterns
  • No Cookies: We don't use tracking cookies or similar technologies
  • No IP Logging: We don't store your IP address or location data

mailEmail Handling

Your privacy is our top priority in email management:

  • Temporary Storage: Emails are stored temporarily and automatically deleted
  • No Content Scanning: We don't read or analyze your email content
  • End-to-End Privacy: Emails are accessible only through your temporary inbox
  • Automatic Deletion: All data is permanently deleted when you generate a new email

shareThird-Party Services

We use minimal third-party services to ensure functionality:

  • Mail.TM API: For temporary email generation and management
  • Google Fonts: For typography (loaded from Google's servers)
  • No Advertising: We don't integrate with any advertising networks

admin_panel_settingsYour Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Right to Access: View all emails in your temporary inbox
  • Right to Delete: Instantly delete your temporary email and all associated data
  • Right to Anonymity: Use our service without providing any personal information
  • Right to Disappear: Your data vanishes when you close the browser or generate a new email

family_restroomChildren's Privacy

Our service is safe for users of all ages:

  • No age verification required
  • No personal information collected from any users
  • Completely anonymous usage
